Listening to the Earth with deep learning.

I am Wu-Yu Liao, a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Earth Sciences, Academia Sinica, Taipei. I build models that detect earthquakes and pick seismic phases on continuous records, and I now apply them to distributed acoustic sensing together with regional seismic networks.

From one phase pick to a monitoring network, and now to fiber.

012019–2022
Phase picking & detection

Deep learning pickers for continuous data

ARRU Phase Picker picks P and S arrivals with an attention recurrent residual U-Net. RED-PAN detects earthquakes and picks phases in real time with a multitask attention network, on continuous data rather than on cut event windows.

  • ARRU Phase Picker · Seismological Research Letters, 2021
  • RED-PAN · IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 2022
  • Distinguished innovation project, TWCC Star Startup Competition, NCHC, 2020

022020–2022
Autonomous networks

From phase probabilities to located events

Backprojecting phase time functions predicted by deep learning detects and locates earthquakes directly on continuous recordings, a step toward seismic networks that run without manual picking. Earlier, GPU acceleration made automatic microseismic monitoring fast enough for a full aftershock sequence.

  • Toward Fully Autonomous Seismic Networks · Seismological Research Letters, 2022
  • GAMMA, applied to the 2019 Ridgecrest sequence · Seismological Research Letters, 2020

032019–2023
Slope failure

Rockfalls and earthquakes in one model

RockNet detects and associates rockfalls and earthquakes through multitask and transfer learning. Related work clusters slope hazard recordings without labels and locates large landslides by waveform back projection.

  • RockNet · IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 2023
  • Unsupervised deep embedded clustering of slope hazard recordings · SRL, 2023
  • Real-time landslide detection by waveform back projection · Geofluids, 2019

042024–2026
Seismotectonics & early warning

Earthquake sequences in Taiwan

A deep learning catalog of the 2025 M6.4 Dapu sequence resolves its seismogenic structure. Related work covers crustal tomography of west-central Taiwan, and hypocenter location and magnitude estimation for earthquake early warning with dense arrays.

  • Seismogenic structure of the 2025 M6.4 Dapu sequence · TAO, 2025
  • Mid-crustal high-velocity body beneath west-central Taiwan · JGR: Solid Earth, 2025
  • Hypocenter location (EPS, 2024) and rapid magnitude estimation (TAO, 2026) for early warning
052026–now
Current · IES, Academia Sinica

Distributed acoustic sensing for seismic monitoring

DAS turns a fiber optic cable into a dense line of seismic sensors, with channels every few meters. I am developing monitoring with DAS records and linking it to the stations of regional seismic networks, so that fiber and conventional stations contribute to one catalog.

  • Phase picking on DAS records
  • Event location
  • Integration with regional seismic networks
